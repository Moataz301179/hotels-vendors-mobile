## Full Description
INVO is the mobile fulfillment app for HotelsVendors — Egypt's first B2B hospitality procurement platform built for hotels and their suppliers.

FOR SUPPLIERS:
• Receive direct purchase orders from hotels in real time
• Manage your product catalog — upload price sheets, AI auto-maps to categories
• Accept and fulfill orders with delivery tracking
• Get paid in 48 hours through integrated reverse factoring (Oliv partner)
• Track earnings, GRN (Goods Received Notes), and factoring history
• Invite hotels you serve — earn network credits and priority cash-out access

FOR HOTELS:
• Browse real supplier catalogs by category (F&B, Housekeeping, FF&E, Linens, Amenities, Engineering, OS&E, Technology)
• Smart search with auto-complete and related product suggestions
• Compare prices across multiple suppliers for the same product
• Get instant delivery quotes to your hotel location
• Raise RFQs (Request for Quota) for volume purchases
• Approve purchase orders through multi-tier authority matrix
• Track orders, invoices, and ETA e-invoicing compliance
• Invite your suppliers to join — both earn rewards

KEY FEATURES:
• Real supplier products only — no fake listings, no stock photos
• ETA e-invoicing integration (Egyptian Tax Authority compliant)
• FRA regulatory shield for factoring transactions
• 48-hour supplier cash-out through Oliv partnership (promo: CHV000)
• Smart procurement engines: complementary products, alternatives, cross-vendor price comparison
• Hotel-location delivery quoting via Egypt carrier corridors (Bosta, Mylerz, Aramex)
• AI assistant for procurement guidance and onboarding
• Multi-property support for hotel groups
• Arabic + English support

FREE during beta. No subscription required. Paid plans unlock after testing phase.

COMPLIANCE:
• ETA e-invoicing clearance
• FRA non-duplication registry checks
• AML/KYC verification for factoring
• InstaPay, Paymob, Fawry payment rails
